## What the AGPL permits

The AGPL permits commercial use, charging for copies or services, modification, and forks, subject
to its conditions. No payment to Matteo Dante is required merely because a use is commercial.
It does not require every application used alongside Filo to become open source.

When conveying a covered work, comply with the applicable license, notice, and Corresponding
Source requirements. Under section 13, a modified version must prominently offer its Corresponding
Source to users interacting with it remotely over a computer network. The obligation is to the
people entitled to receive source under the license, not automatically to publish every internal
change on a public website. Private use alone does not create a general publication obligation.
The license itself controls; whether a combined product is a covered work depends on the facts.

A proprietary distribution or modified hosted offering that cannot meet the applicable AGPL
conditions needs separate permission from the relevant rightsholders. A separate process, API,
or service boundary does not by itself settle that question.

This model protects against non-compliant proprietary reuse. It does not prohibit a compliant
commercial competitor or fork, guarantee exclusivity, or make ideas and functionality proprietary.
Existing lawful AGPL grants remain available under their terms; this policy does not withdraw them.

## Third-party limits in the current package

**The current package is not cleared for unrestricted commercial reuse as a whole.** In particular,
`src/filo/wellbeing.py` embeds WHO-5 questionnaire text subject to separate terms including a
non-commercial restriction. Filo's AGPL grant and any agreement with Matteo Dante cannot override
those rights. Commercial use involving that material requires the relevant rightsholders'
permission, or a deliverable that actually omits the restricted material. Merely disabling the
feature while shipping its text does not establish clearance.

The original Filo material is open source. Do not describe every bundled item as AGPL-licensed
or the mixed package as having no non-commercial restrictions. The third-party notices identify
these exceptions; dependencies and downloaded models also retain their own terms.

## Contributions

Contributors keep their copyright. New contributions intended for inclusion need a DCO sign-off
and explicit acceptance of the [Filo Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md), following
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). The CLA gives Matteo Dante non-exclusive rights for Filo's
open-source and commercial licensing and commits him to keeping accepted contributions available
under AGPL-3.0-or-later. It does not assign ownership or promise revenue sharing.

This does not retroactively relicense earlier contributions. Before including any earlier or
third-party contribution in a proprietary deliverable, establish the necessary rights separately.
A bot author, AI attribution, or commit sign-off alone is not proof of ownership or relicensing rights.
