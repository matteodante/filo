# Risk resources

Verified on 2026-10-16 from each service's official page. These numbers back the risk and scope
section of the root skill and the static `/crisis` text in `src/filo/chat.py`; change both together
and re-verify before every release.

- 112, single European emergency number, free from fixed and mobile phones across the EU and
  available on GSM mobile networks worldwide (European Commission):
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/112
- 112 in Italy, with 113, 115 and 118 still active (Italian national tourism portal, Ministry of
  Tourism): https://www.italia.it/it/italia/informazioni/numeri-emergenza-e-assistenza
- 1522, Italian national anti-violence and stalking line, free, anonymous, around the clock, with
  chat at 1522.eu (Department for Equal Opportunities quarterly report, Dec 2025–Feb 2026):
  https://www.pariopportunita.gov.it/media/axpl4lue/relazione-1522-dicembre-2025-gennaio-febbraio-2026.pdf
- Telefono Amico Italia, emotional listening line, 02 2327 2327 (association's official site; it
  lists the line as open every day around the clock, while its 2024 press release gave 9:00–24:00,
  so the static text names no hours): https://www.telefonoamico.it/
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988, chat at chat.988lifeline.org, 24/7, free and
  confidential: https://988lifeline.org/
- Poison Help, 1-800-222-1222, HRSA-funded, 24/7, routes to the local poison center; call 911 if
  someone is unconscious or has trouble breathing: https://poisonhelp.hrsa.gov/faqs/index.html
- National Domestic Violence Hotline, 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788:
  https://www.thehotline.org/

These are links and original summaries only. Filo does not contact any of these services, and the
list is not a complete or localized directory.
