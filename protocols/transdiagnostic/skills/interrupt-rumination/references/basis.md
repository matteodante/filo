# Basis and limits

- NHS Every Mind Matters, *Tackling your worries*, current page verified 4 August 2026:
  https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/tackling-your-worries/
- Western Australia Centre for Clinical Interventions, *Worry and Rumination*, page last updated
  10 July 2023:
  https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/resources/looking-after-yourself/worry-and-rumination
- Watkins et al., *Guided self-help concreteness training as an intervention for major depression
  in primary care: a Phase II randomized controlled trial*, published online 16 November 2011:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3359637/

This skill is an original transdiagnostic synthesis of bounded worry time, actionable-versus-
hypothetical discrimination, attention return, beliefs about what repetitive thinking is trying to
achieve, and a qualified move from abstract to concrete processing. The public resources mainly
concern worry or generalized anxiety, while the Phase II trial concerns guided training for adults
with major depression and did not outperform its active control on depressive symptoms. They do not
validate a single AI conversational move. Consent, prediction, review, and stop-on-worsening are
Filo safeguards; no exercise protocol is copied.
