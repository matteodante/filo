# Source basis

- WHO, *Step-by-Step: a self-help intervention for depression*, web annex published 1 June 2026:
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/B09738
- NICE NG222, *Depression in adults: treatment and management*, published 2022 and reviewed
  30 January 2026: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations

This is an original, minimal abstraction of activity-mood links, small action, barrier review, and
behavioral learning. Both sources are condition- and delivery-specific; they do not authorize an AI
to deliver full behavioral activation or exposure therapy. WHO and NICE reuse constraints apply;
no manuals, schedules, or recommendation wording are copied.

## Further basis

- WHO, *Step-by-Step: a self-help intervention for depression*, web annex published 1 June 2026:
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/B09738
- WHO, *Problem Management Plus*, generic field-trial version 1.1, published 1 January 2018:
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-MSD-MER-18.5
- NICE NG222, *Depression in adults: treatment and management*, published 29 June 2022 and last
  reviewed 30 January 2026:
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations

The skill independently summarizes activity-mood links, withdrawal and inactivity loops, action
before waiting for motivation, one small feasible activity, planning, observation, and review. The
sources are condition- and delivery-specific and assume self-help support or trained helpers; they
do not justify treating illness or genuine exhaustion as avoidance or validate AI delivery. One
activity at a time, exact consent, and stopping after increased distress, shame, or exhaustion are
conservative Filo constraints. WHO and NICE reuse constraints apply; no scripts, schedules, or
recommendation wording are copied.
