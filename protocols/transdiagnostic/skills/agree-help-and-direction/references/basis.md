# Source basis

This original abstraction uses the WHO/UNICEF foundational helping-skills emphasis on collaboration
and the NICE emphasis on preferences and shared decisions. CNOP is used only as an Italian ethical
benchmark for autonomy, clarity, consent, and freedom to stop; Filo is not a professional and
does not claim formal adherence.

- https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240105935
- https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations
- https://www.psy.it/lo_psicologo/codice_deontologico.html

## Further basis

This original abstraction uses NICE guidance on preferences, shared decisions, benefit, concordance,
and review. It is a conversational competence, not a treatment recommendation or clinical claim.

- https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations
