# Commercial licensing enquiries

Filo remains available under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Commercial use that complies with that license
does not require a separate agreement with Matteo Dante. See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for
scope and third-party exceptions.

If you want to distribute a proprietary derivative or operate a modified service without meeting
the applicable AGPL obligations, contact Matteo Dante through a
[licensing enquiry on GitHub](https://github.com/matteodante/filo/issues/new).
Describe the intended use, the version or commit, distribution or hosting model, and the rights
you need. Keep confidential business information and personal data out of public issues; request
a private channel before sharing them.

## A separate agreement is required

This page grants no proprietary license, exemption, price, warranty, or right to support.
Only a separate written agreement with the authorized rightsholder can grant those rights.
It must identify the parties, covered version and material, permitted uses, fees if any,
third-party exclusions, notices, and applicable contract terms. Negotiations do not suspend the
AGPL requirements for anyone relying on the AGPL.

Matteo Dante may license only material he owns or has authority to sublicense. An agreement
cannot revoke existing AGPL rights, change other rightsholders' terms, or bind contributors
who have not granted the necessary rights.

## Before offering a commercial deliverable

The maintainer must record, for the exact deliverable:

1. The original material covered and the evidence of rights, including applicable contributor
   acceptances. Existing DCO-only contributions require a separate rights check; do not infer a
   proprietary grant from silence, a Git author name, or an automated contribution.
2. Every third-party component and applicable notice or permission. The WHO-5 text currently in
   `src/filo/wellbeing.py` is not covered by Filo's commercial offer. Obtain the relevant permissions
   (including translation rights where applicable), or omit that material from the actual artifact.
3. Compliance with the CLA's commitment to make accepted contributions available under AGPL.
4. A review of the contract and the intended commercial activity, including the alpha's current
   non-commercial operating assumptions in [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md) and the linked assessments.

There is currently no pre-cleared commercial package or standard customer contract. This policy
establishes a route to negotiate one; it does not certify the current mixed package for commercial
use. Commercial licensing does not introduce a hosted service, paid support, or a medical claim.
